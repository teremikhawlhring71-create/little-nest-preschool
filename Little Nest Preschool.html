💗 About Our School

A happy beginning for every little learner.

Welcome to Little Nest Preschool

Little Nest Preschool is a caring learning environment for young children aged 2–6 years, located in Dawrpui, Aizawl, Mizoram.

We believe children learn best when they feel safe, happy, respected and encouraged to explore the world around them.

Our focus is on building confidence, independence, friendships and a love of learning.

❤️

Our Mission

To provide a safe and nurturing environment that supports every child's development.

🌟

Our Vision

To help children become confident, curious and happy learners.

🤝

Our Values

Kindness, respect, creativity, inclusion and partnership with families.

🩹 Accidents & Incidents

Keeping Children Safe

The safety and wellbeing of children are important to us. Any accident or incident involving a child should be recorded and communicated to the parent or guardian.

  • Staff should respond promptly and provide appropriate care.
  • Parents or guardians should be informed about significant incidents.
  • Emergency assistance should be sought when necessary.
  • Accidents and incidents should be documented appropriately.

For details about the school's current procedures, please contact the administration.

🚪 Dropping Off & Collection

Safe Arrival and Collection

  • Parents and guardians should follow the school's arrival and collection arrangements.
  • Children should be handed over to an authorised staff member.
  • Children should only be collected by a parent, guardian or person authorised by the family.
  • Inform the school in advance if collection arrangements change.
  • Keep contact information up to date.

Please speak to the school about its authorised collection procedure.

🍎 Healthy Eating

Encouraging Healthy Habits

Healthy food and good eating habits support children's growth and wellbeing.

  • Encourage balanced and nutritious food choices.
  • Provide access to drinking water.
  • Inform staff about allergies and dietary requirements.
  • Encourage children to develop independence during meals.
  • Maintain clean and hygienic eating areas.

🚽 Potty Training

Supporting Every Child

Children develop toileting independence at different ages and at different rates. We aim to support each child with patience, respect and encouragement.

  • Work together with parents and guardians.
  • Respect each child's individual needs and readiness.
  • Encourage good hygiene and handwashing.
  • Maintain the child's privacy and dignity.
  • Communicate any concerns with parents.

🤒 Sickness

Health and Wellbeing

Parents should inform the school if their child is unwell or has a contagious illness.

  • Children who are unwell should remain at home when necessary.
  • Parents should inform the school about relevant symptoms or diagnoses.
  • Staff should contact parents if a child becomes unwell during the day.
  • Medical emergencies should receive immediate attention.
  • Return-to-school arrangements should follow the school's health policy and appropriate medical advice.
